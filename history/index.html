1966
Tamariki School was founded by a group of parents and teachers, and Tamariki School Incorporated was formed
1967
After much discussion and fundraising, the school opened, located on England Street in a rented band hall
1967
June Bent became Tamariki's first Principal
1968
During the first year of the school children developed the meeting and request systems which are core to decision making and conflict resolution
1969
Tamariki moved to a house at 83 Rutherford Street, artwork by Hiliare Campbell
1975
Full registration of the school in 1975 gave some limited access to Government funding
1975
Pat Edwards became Tamariki School's second Principal
1990
Tamariki, which had been a private school, became a state integrated school.
1993
After years of fundraising the Society purchased land and designed and built the present school at St Johns Street
1996
Springhill opened in 1996 to take the children Tamariki could not fit, with Bob Gibbons as its Principal
2002
Pat Edwards retired, and Di Scullin became Tamariki School's third Principal
2017
Tamariki celebrated its 50th Jubilee
2021
Di Scullin retired, and Samuel Smith became Tamariki School's fourth Principal
2024
Blake Michell became Tamariki Schools fifth Principal
